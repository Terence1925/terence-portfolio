01 / 03

QC-SSDD Service Tracker

A full-stack benefits-management platform built for the Quezon City Social Services Development Department, delivered through Sparksoft Solutions — replacing manual, paper-based tracking of social service transactions.

  • Role-based approval workflows across departments
  • 30+ reporting endpoints synced to Google BigQuery
  • AWS S3 documents, JWT auth, full audit trails

The problem

Social workers tracked benefit applications and household data manually, with no shared system for approvals, reporting, or field data collection during disaster response.

What we built

  • Role-based transaction lifecycle management with multi-level approval workflows
  • Household and informant registry with deduplication and audit history
  • Mobile field-data intake via ODK integration — GPS location and e-signature capture
  • Disaster-relief module tracking flood/typhoon assistance by district
  • 30+ reporting and analytics endpoints, synced to Google BigQuery for department-wide dashboards
  • AWS S3 document storage, JWT authentication, and full audit trails

My role

Engineer on a small Sparksoft team — built and shipped frontend features and backend fixes across the transaction, reporting, and disaster-relief modules, with rigorous test coverage as part of the team's TDD workflow.
